# ⛏️ Minecraft Stuff

> **A completely unnecessary Minecraft toolkit that somehow became necessary.**

Welcome to **Minecraft Stuff**, the website that answers the questions Minecraft players have been asking since approximately 2011:

- "Am I about to die?"
- "How many blocks do I need?"
- "What does this redstone thing actually do?"
- "Can I legally prosecute Steve?"
- "Why did my entire base explode?"
- "How many diamonds did that idiot steal?"
- "What potion should I make?"
- "Why am I still playing this game?"

Minecraft Stuff has answers.

Some of them are even useful.

---

## 🟩 What Is This?

Minecraft Stuff is an all-in-one companion for Minecraft survival.

It contains a collection of extremely serious, highly advanced, definitely government-approved tools for surviving the block game.

### Current arsenal

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ **How Screwed Am I?** | Determines how catastrophically your current situation is going |
| ⚖️ **Minecraft Court** | Brings server criminals to justice |
| ⚡ **Redstone Encyclopedia** | Explains why red dust suddenly became computer science |
| 🧪 **Potion Lab** | Helps you brew suspicious liquids |
| 🔎 **Block Detective** | Inspects Minecraft screenshots |
| ⚔️ **Enchantment Builder** | Helps optimize your equipment |
| ⛏️ **Crafting Calculator** | Counts blocks because apparently counting to 64 is difficult |
| 🏗️ **Build Planner** | Generates layer-by-layer building blueprints |
| 🏆 **Advancement Tracker** | Tracks your progress toward becoming unnecessarily accomplished |

## ⚠️ How Screwed Am I?

Enter your survival statistics:

- ❤️ Health
- 🛡️ Armor
- 🍖 Food
- 👾 Nearby hostile mobs
- 📍 Distance to danger
- 💎 Diamonds
- 🎒 Inventory usage

Then press:

> **ASSESS MY FATE**

The system will analyze your situation and provide a completely scientific threat assessment.

If you have 2 hearts, no armor, three creepers nearby, and 47 diamonds in your inventory:

**Congratulations.**

You have invented a new category of poor decision-making.

---

## ⚖️ Minecraft Court

Has someone stolen your diamonds?

Did someone destroy your house?

Did Steve commit crimes against humanity with a flint and steel?

File a case.

Minecraft Court allows you to enter:

- Defendant
- Crime
- Evidence

The court will then deliver a verdict with all the professionalism you'd expect from a legal system operated by Minecraft players.

---

## ⚡ Redstone Encyclopedia

Redstone is basically electricity.

Except:

- it is dust,
- it makes no sense,
- villagers somehow interact with it,
- and someone will eventually build a working computer with it.

The Redstone Encyclopedia explains components and provides demonstrations so you can finally understand what those weird glowing rocks are doing.

## 🧪 Potion Lab

Choose an effect.

Choose a modifier.

Press **BREW POTION**.

Congratulations.

You are now an alchemist.

Please do not drink anything in real life because this README does not cover that.

## 🔎 Block Detective

Upload a Minecraft screenshot and inspect what is inside it.

Because sometimes you look at a screenshot and think:

> "What block is that?"

And instead of searching the internet like a normal person, you can use an entire application dedicated to answering it.

Technology has peaked.
